Our Mission

Bridging the Gap Between Surplus and Need

FoodBridge is on a mission to reduce food waste and hunger by creating a simple, efficient platform that connects food donors with NGOs, orphanages, and communities in need.

Why We Exist

Every day, tons of perfectly good food goes to waste while millions of people go hungry. This disconnect between surplus and need inspired us to create FoodBridge.

We believe that no one should go hungry when there's excess food available. Our platform makes it easy for restaurants, event planners, grocery stores, and individuals to donate surplus food to those who need it most.

Since our launch, we've facilitated thousands of food donations, connecting generous donors with orphanages, shelters, and community organizations.
